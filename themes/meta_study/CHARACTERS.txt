meta_study - THE CHARACTER SHOWCASE
===================================

This theme has an empty slot for a character, and it is empty on purpose.

WE SHIP NO CHARACTER ART. Not a sprite, not a silhouette, not a frame of
anything. What the theme draws today is a placeholder: a plain standing capsule
in walnut with a thin brass edge, sitting behind the textbox on the right side
of the stage, with a soft warm glow breathing behind it. It is a shape and a
gradient - arithmetic, nothing more.

Put your favourite character there instead. Two ways:


1. DRAG AND DROP (the easy way)
-------------------------------
Open the theme editor, click the showcase element to select it, and drag any
GIF or animated WebP from your file manager straight onto it. The editor copies
the file into this theme's asyncao/media/ folder, adds the [media] entry with
its size and hash, and switches the element over to it for you.

The element is deliberately NOT locked (locked = no), so you can also drag its
corners to resize it, or drag it somewhere else entirely.


2. BY HAND (if you would rather edit the file)
----------------------------------------------
Put your animation in this folder under asyncao/media/, then add two things to
asyncao_theme.ini.

  a) a [media] section naming it (the size and hash are optional - a bare file
     name is legal, they just let AsyncAO budget and verify before decoding):

       [media]
       mychar = asyncao/media/mychar.webp

  b) in [element.showcase], change the kind and point it at that id:

       kind  = image
       media = mychar

     Leave `fit = contain` if you want the whole character to fit inside the
     box without distortion. Use `fit = cover` to fill the box and crop, or
     `fit = stretch` to squash it to the rect exactly.

Everything else in [element.showcase] keeps working: `rect` still follows the
textbox wherever a layout puts it, `opacity` still dims it, and `loop = yes`
plays an animation forever.


NOTES
-----
* Paths must stay inside this theme folder. Absolute paths, "..", and symlinks
  are refused by the reader - that is deliberate, and it is what makes a theme
  safe to share.
* The showcase's bottom edge is pinned exactly to the top edge of the textbox,
  so your character is cropped by the box the way a visual novel crops a sprite.
  If you want them standing clear of it, give the element a smaller height.
* Whatever you drop in is YOUR file and your responsibility. If you re-share
  this theme with art inside it, make sure you have the right to do that - the
  theme as we ship it is entirely original and carries no such condition.
