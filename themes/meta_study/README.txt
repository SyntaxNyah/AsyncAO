meta_study - a standalone theme for AsyncAO

A sealed mansion study at dusk: walnut wainscot, damask going dark on the wall,
two sconces breathing at the edges of sight, and one flat black translucent
textbox floating in front of it all - reproduced from its own measured geometry
rather than imitated. Serif body text, a humanist nameplate, red truth.

INSTALL: drop this folder into your themes/ folder, then pick "meta_study" in
Settings. Nothing else to do. See CHARACTERS.txt for the character showcase.

ALL ART IS PROCEDURAL. This theme ships zero bytes of image, font or audio:
every surface is a palette colour, a shape, a gradient, a generator tile or
plain text. It is original work by the AsyncAO project, AGPLv3-compatible.
