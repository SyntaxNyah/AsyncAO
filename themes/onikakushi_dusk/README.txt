Onikakushi Dusk — an AsyncAO theme (format 1)

A village at dusk: a violet zenith falling through rose and orange to gold, hill
and powerline silhouettes along a horizon band no widget ever covers, a wide
translucent reading band across the stage, and one three-pixel red rule down the
speaking edge of the box for the undertow under all that calm.

Install: drop this folder into your themes/ folder, then pick "Onikakushi Dusk"
in Settings > Theme. Nothing else to unpack.

All art is procedural. This theme ships one INI and this README — no images, no
fonts, no audio, not one byte of anybody else's work. Every gradient, tile,
silhouette and rule is computed by the client at load time from the colours and
proportions written in asyncao_theme.ini. AGPLv3-compatible, reuse and edit
freely.
