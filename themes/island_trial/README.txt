island_trial - a standalone AsyncAO theme.

A trial held in daylight. Pale sky over bleached sand, frond hairlines hanging
past the corners, surf breaking on the speech scrim, and one violet-magenta wire
that was never bleached at all - because in the sun it stops being decoration.

INSTALL: drop this folder into your themes/ folder, then pick "Island Trial" in
Settings -> Theme. Nothing else to do.

ALL ART IS PROCEDURAL. This folder contains three text files and nothing else -
no images, no fonts, no audio. Every surface you see is a palette colour, a shape,
a gradient, a generator tile or plain text, computed at load time. See
CHARACTERS.txt to put your own character beside the nameplate.
