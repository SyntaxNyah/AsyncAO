island_trial - THE CHARACTER SHOWCASE
=====================================

This theme keeps one slot beside the nameplate for a character of your choosing:

    [element.showcase]

Out of the box it is a placeholder - a standing figure-shaped plate in the
theme's ink with a thin magenta edge, rising out of the speech scrim
immediately to the right of the speaker's name and standing on the scrim's top
edge, lit from behind by a soft violet glow. It is a stand-in. It is meant to
be replaced.

WE SHIP NO CHARACTER ART. Not one byte. The showcase is a shape and a glow,
both computed at load time, so there is nothing here to be anyone's likeness.
What goes in it is entirely yours.


THE EASY WAY - DRAG AND DROP
----------------------------
1. Open Settings -> Theme -> Edit theme.
2. Drag any GIF, animated WebP, PNG or WebP file from your desktop and drop it
   onto the showcase figure beside the nameplate.
3. That is it. The editor copies the file into this folder under
   asyncao/media/, writes the [media] entry with its size and hash, switches the
   element to kind = image, and keeps the fit and the rect it already had.

Animated files animate. A GIF or an animated WebP plays on the element's own
clock (this one is clock 2), so you can slow it down or speed it up in the
inspector without touching anything else in the theme.


THE MANUAL WAY - BY HAND
------------------------
1. Put your file in this folder under asyncao/media/, e.g.
       themes/island_trial/asyncao/media/mychar.webp
2. Add a [media] section to asyncao_theme.ini (bytes and hash are optional - a
   bare file name is legal):

       [media]
       mychar = asyncao/media/mychar.webp

3. In [element.showcase], change

       kind      = shape        ->   kind  = image
       (and add)                     media = mychar

   Leave `fit = contain` as it is: it makes any aspect ratio fit the slot
   without stretching. Delete `fill`, `stroke` and `stroke_px` if you want the
   plate behind your art gone, or keep them as a card behind a cut-out sprite.

Paths are theme-relative, forward-slashed, and may never leave this folder: no
absolute paths, no "..", no symlinks. That is enforced on load, not on trust.


SIZE, POSITION AND LIGHT
------------------------
- `rect = 52, -190, 74, 175` is a DELTA on the NAMEPLATE's rect, not an
  absolute box. On the stock 46 x 15 name tag it resolves to 120 x 190 standing
  at (53, 170) - beside the name, on the scrim's top edge. Widen the figure by
  raising the third number (94 gives you 140 px wide); raise it by making the
  second number more negative, which also makes it taller unless you drop the
  fourth number by the same amount.
- It is anchored to the nameplate ON PURPOSE: the figure then follows the name
  wherever a layout puts the speech box, and it can never cover the speaker's
  own name, which is drawn before this band and would be eaten by an opaque
  element sitting on it.
- The element is `locked = no`, so you can just drag and resize it in the
  editor instead of doing that arithmetic.
- [element.showcase_glow] is the violet halo behind it. Retint it to suit your
  character - it is one `tint=` in its gen_params - or widen it by the same
  delta arithmetic to make the light spill further.
- Keep the bottom edge on the scrim's top edge if you want the figure cropped
  the way a visual novel crops a sprite on the box edge. Raise it and the
  figure simply stands clear on the stage.


LICENSING
---------
Whatever you drop in is yours to be responsible for. This theme is
AGPLv3-compatible because everything in it is original and procedural; a
character image you add is neither, so do not redistribute the folder with
someone else's art inside it unless you have the right to.
