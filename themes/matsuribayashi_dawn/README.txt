Festival Dawn (matsuribayashi_dawn) — an AsyncAO theme.

The resolution register: gold sky lifting off the horizon, warm paper chrome, a
shoji-lattice dialogue band settling into tatami, and one quiet gold mark on the
nameplate. Light where its dusk siblings are dark, and hopeful on purpose.

Install: drop this folder into your themes/ folder, then pick "Festival Dawn" in
Settings. Nothing else to do.

All art is procedural. Every surface is computed at load from a palette, flat
shapes, two-stop gradients and six generated tiles — zero bytes of bundled art,
zero bundled fonts, zero bundled audio, no third-party assets. Colours and
proportions were measured from reference frames; none of that imagery is
reproduced, traced or embedded here. AGPLv3-compatible.
