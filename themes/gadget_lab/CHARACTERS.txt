gadget_lab - THE CHARACTER SHOWCASE
===================================

This theme has a polaroid pinned to the top-right corner of the stage, and the
photo in it is empty on purpose.

WE SHIP NO CHARACTER ART. Not a sprite, not a silhouette, not a frame of
anything. What the theme draws today is a placeholder: a standing capsule in
smoky glass-brown with a thin amber edge, seated on the bottom of the photo
well, with a soft warm glow breathing behind the whole card. It is a shape and
a gradient - arithmetic, nothing more.

Put your favourite character there instead. Two ways:


1. DRAG AND DROP (the easy way)
-------------------------------
Open the theme editor, click the showcase element to select it, and drag any
GIF or animated WebP from your file manager straight onto it. The editor copies
the file into this theme's asyncao/media/ folder, adds the [media] entry with
its size and hash, and switches the element over to it for you.

The element is deliberately NOT locked (locked = no), so you can also drag its
corners to resize it, or drag it somewhere else entirely.


2. BY HAND (if you would rather edit the file)
----------------------------------------------
Put your animation in this folder under asyncao/media/, then add two things to
asyncao_theme.ini.

  a) a [media] section naming it (the size and hash are optional - a bare file
     name is legal, they just let AsyncAO budget and verify before decoding):

       [media]
       mychar = asyncao/media/mychar.webp

  b) in [element.showcase], change the kind and point it at that id:

       kind  = image
       media = mychar

     Leave `fit = contain` if you want the whole character to fit inside the
     photo well without distortion. Use `fit = cover` to fill the well and crop,
     or `fit = stretch` to squash it to the rect exactly.

Everything else in [element.showcase] keeps working: `rect` still follows the
stage wherever a layout puts it, `opacity` still dims it, and `loop = yes`
plays an animation forever.


THE REST OF THE POLAROID
------------------------
The card is five more elements around yours, and you can edit any of them:

  showcase_glow    the warm halo behind the card
  showcase_card    the paper itself (a two-stop gradient)
  showcase_well    the photo window your character sits in
  showcase         >>> YOURS <<<
  showcase_cap     the caption in the chin - change the text to a name
  showcase_frame   the rust keyline round the card

If you want a bigger picture, grow showcase_well and showcase together. Their
rects are anchored to the viewport and written as deltas, so the numbers in the
file are "how far from the stage's own edges", not absolute pixels - which is
why the whole card stays in the corner at any window size.

The card is NOT tilted. A tilt would need the same `rot` on four separate
elements rotating about four different centres, and the seams come apart. If
you want it anyway, the file says exactly where to put `rot = 3`.


NOTES
-----
* Paths must stay inside this theme folder. Absolute paths, "..", and symlinks
  are refused by the reader - that is deliberate, and it is what makes a theme
  safe to share.
* Whatever you drop in is YOUR file and your responsibility. If you re-share
  this theme with art inside it, make sure you have the right to do that - the
  theme as we ship it is entirely original and carries no such condition.
