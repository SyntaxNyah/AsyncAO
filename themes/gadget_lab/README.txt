gadget_lab - a standalone theme for AsyncAO

A hand-built lab bench at 3am: a warm-black void, a tan perfboard deck bolted
across the top with a small cold-cathode digit counter glowing on it, rust
framing on every panel, one amber rule under the dialogue, and a pale polaroid
pinned to the corner of the stage. Technical gothic for everything you read,
mono for everything that counts.

INSTALL: drop this folder into your themes/ folder, then pick "gadget_lab" in
Settings. Nothing else to do. See CHARACTERS.txt for the character showcase.

ALL ART IS PROCEDURAL. This theme ships zero bytes of image, font or audio:
every surface is a palette colour, a shape, a gradient, a generator tile or
plain text. It is original work by the AsyncAO project, AGPLv3-compatible.
