v3_dark - a standalone AsyncAO theme.

The coldest of the trial rooms: a near-black void, two families of neon hairline
crossing the whole canvas past every edge, a checkered floor disc under the
stage light, and one magenta rule on a speech box that has no frame at all.
Everything else is a thin brutal rule - 1 px, axis-aligned, dead cold grey.

INSTALL: drop this folder into your themes/ folder, then pick "V3 Dark" in
Settings -> Theme. Nothing else to do.

ALL ART IS PROCEDURAL. This folder holds two text files and nothing else - no
images, no fonts, no audio, nothing traced or copied. Every surface is a palette
colour, a shape, a gradient, a generator tile or plain text, computed at load
from ten tile recipes and five colours.
