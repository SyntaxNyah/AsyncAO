purgatorio - a standalone AsyncAO theme.

A witches' court sitting in red: a drape wall of wine and rose either side of the
stage, gold sigil medallions repeating across the floor, one hairline magic circle
turning slowly over the scene, and a figure centre-stage behind the gilt frame.

INSTALL: drop this folder into your themes/ folder, then pick "Purgatorio" in
Settings -> Theme. Nothing else to do.

ALL ART IS PROCEDURAL. This folder contains three text files and nothing else -
no images, no fonts, no audio. Every surface you see is a palette colour, a shape,
a gradient, a generator tile or plain text, computed at load time. See
CHARACTERS.txt to put your own character on the stage.
