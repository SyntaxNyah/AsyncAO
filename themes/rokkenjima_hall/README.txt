Rokkenjima Hall - a standalone theme for AsyncAO

The mansion's main hall, drawn as a straight-on elevation: walnut pilasters
standing between three wall bays, a gold-tan moulding round every panel, a
crimson runner with a gilt medallion lattice down the middle aisle, drapes
flanking the stage, and one chandelier hanging just off the top of the frame
whose light is the only light in the room. Stately serif chrome throughout.

INSTALL: drop this folder into your AsyncAO themes/ folder, then pick
"Rokkenjima Hall" in Settings. Nothing else to do.

ALL ART IS PROCEDURAL. This theme ships zero bytes of image, font or audio:
every surface is a palette colour, a shape, a gradient, a generator tile or
plain text, generated at runtime from the numbers in asyncao_theme.ini.
Original work by the AsyncAO project, AGPLv3-compatible.
