Watanagashi Matsuri - an AsyncAO theme.

Festival night: lantern gold strung over deep summer blue, a wide dark
translucent chatbox with a gold hairline down both edges, a bigger stage for the
scene, and corners that stay too dark for a festival. Festive, and not quite at
ease.

Install: drop this folder into your themes/ folder, then pick "Watanagashi
Matsuri" in Settings > Theme. Nothing else to do.

All art is procedural. There are no image files, no fonts and no audio in this
folder - the whole look is four generated tiles, flat shapes, two-stop gradients
and two lines of original text, computed at load. Nothing here is copied,
traced, sampled or derived from anyone else's artwork. Licence: procedural,
AGPLv3-compatible.
