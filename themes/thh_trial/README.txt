Trial Floor — an AsyncAO theme
A monochrome courtroom crossed by glowing magenta hairlines, standing on a warm
gold spoke floor with a black-and-white checker medallion at its centre. Cyan is
the second voice, one red is kept back for alerts, and a lit podium waits behind
the chat box.

INSTALL: drop this folder into your AsyncAO themes/ folder, then pick
"Trial Floor" in Settings > Theme. Nothing else to do.

THE PODIUM: the empty stand on the field is yours to fill — see CHARACTERS.txt.

ALL ART IS PROCEDURAL. This theme ships zero bytes of image, font or audio: every
surface is generated at runtime from the colours, shapes and numbers in
asyncao_theme.ini. Original work, AGPLv3-compatible.
