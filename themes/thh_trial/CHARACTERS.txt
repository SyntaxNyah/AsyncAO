TRIAL FLOOR — the podium showcase
=================================

There is a lit stand on the field, dead centre, directly behind the chat box.
Right now it holds a placeholder: a plain rounded silhouette in a magenta pool
of light, with the caption "your character here".

That stand is yours. Put your favourite character at the podium.

WE SHIP NO CHARACTER ART. Not one byte. This theme is entirely procedural —
palette values, flat shapes, two-stop gradients and ten generated tiles — and
that includes the placeholder, which is a rectangle with round corners and a
1 px keyline. Nothing in this folder is traced from, copied from, or derived
from anyone's artwork, and the showcase exists precisely so you can supply your
own instead of us supplying someone else's.


THE EASY WAY — drag and drop
----------------------------
1. Open AsyncAO and go to Settings > Theme > Open Theme Editor.
2. Pick "Trial Floor" and find the element named `showcase` in the left rail.
3. Drag a GIF or an animated WebP out of your file manager and drop it onto the
   stand on the canvas.

That is it. The editor copies your file into this theme's own media folder,
registers it, and switches the element over for you. Animated files animate.
Save, and it travels with the theme.

The element ships UNLOCKED (`locked = no`) on purpose, so you can also drag it
around, resize it, and put it somewhere else entirely. It is anchored to the
viewport, so it keeps its place on the field if a layout preset resizes the
stage.


THE MANUAL WAY — edit the INI
-----------------------------
1. Put your file at:   asyncao/media/podium.webp
   (create the folders if they are not there; AO2-Client never looks inside
   `asyncao/`, which is why media lives there)

2. Add it to the [media] section of asyncao_theme.ini, which ships empty:

       [media]
       showcase_art = asyncao/media/podium.webp

   The full form is `id = file, bytes, xxhash64`. The size and hash are optional
   and let an importer budget and verify the file before decoding it, which is
   worth doing if you ever share the theme.

3. In [element.showcase], change two lines:

       kind  = image          ; was: shape
       media = showcase_art

   and delete the now-unused `shape`, `fill`, `stroke` and `stroke_px` lines.
   Add `fit = contain` if you want the whole figure to fit inside the stand
   without cropping, or `fit = cover` to fill it and crop the overflow.

4. Delete [element.showcase_hint] to remove the "your character here" caption.

Reload the theme (the reload button in the courtroom, or restart) and there
they are.


WHERE IT SITS, AND WHY
----------------------
The stand resolves to 272 x 128 in design space, centred on the field with its
foot on the chat box's top edge — so whoever stands there is cut off at the
waist by the box, which is what makes them read as standing BEHIND it rather
than pasted on top of it.

That is also the busiest spot on the stage: the live character sprite is drawn
in the same place, and the showcase paints over it. That is deliberate for a
placeholder and a matter of taste for a real one. If your server's sprites are
wide and you would rather not cover them, drag the element to one side in the
editor — the reference this theme is built on puts speakers at the EDGES of the
field, so a stand at x 20 or x 470 is just as authentic as the centre one.

The magenta pool behind it (`showcase_glow`) is a separate element. Move the
stand and the pool stays put unless you move it too; delete the pool if you
would rather your art sat on the bare field.


FORMATS AND SIZES
-----------------
Animated GIF, animated WebP, PNG, WebP and AVIF all work. 272 x 128 is a wide
slot, so a bust or an upper-body crop fits it better than a full standing
sprite — or make the element taller in the editor and use the whole figure.
Anything much bigger than about 1024 x 512 is wasted: it will be scaled down to
the stand, and oversized art eats the theme's media budget, which is shared
across everything in the theme.

Large animations are frame-decimated to fit that budget. If yours matters at
full frame rate, set `decimate = no` on the element — it is honoured only when
the undecimated art still fits.


A NOTE ON WHOSE ART IT IS
-------------------------
Whatever you drop in there is your business, but it becomes part of the theme
folder, and the theme folder is what gets zipped when you export or share. If
you did not make the art, do not redistribute it. Keep it local, or ask the
artist. The theme itself is procedural and AGPLv3-compatible; the moment you
add a file, that file's licence is the one that matters.
