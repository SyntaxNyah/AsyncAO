MEAKASHI FROST — a standalone AsyncAO theme

The cold register: night-blue measured off a frozen shrine, square corners, and
rules exactly one pixel wide. One saturated colour in the whole composition (an
indigo accent), and horror-red held in reserve — it appears on the damage bars
and for the length of a shout, and nowhere else.

INSTALL: drop this folder into your themes/ folder, then pick "Meakashi Frost"
in Settings > Theme. A showcase strip sits beside the IC log with a placeholder
in it — see CHARACTERS.txt to put your own character there.

ALL ART IS PROCEDURAL. No bundled images, fonts or audio: every surface is
computed at load from a palette, flat shapes, gradients and four generated
tiles. Nothing is copied, traced or derived from anyone's artwork.
