Requiem (Chiru) — an AsyncAO theme
Cold near-black under thin gold: a lit stage between two dark rails, rain in two
layers, one candle, and the textbox at its most translucent — flat black at 70%,
with no border, no keyline and no ornament at all, because that is what the
measurements say it is.

INSTALL: drop this folder into your AsyncAO themes/ folder, then pick
"Requiem (Chiru)" in Settings. Nothing else to do.

ALL ART IS PROCEDURAL. This theme ships zero bytes of image, font or audio: every
surface is generated at runtime from the colours, proportions and numbers in
asyncao_theme.ini. Original work, AGPLv3-compatible.
